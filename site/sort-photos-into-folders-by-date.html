How to sort photos into folders by date

A folder with thousands of photos named IMG_2041.JPG is hard to browse. Putting them into one folder per month makes any photo findable. Here is how to do that automatically, without uploading your photos anywhere.

Sort photos into monthly folders

  1. Copy the photos from your camera or phone into a folder on your computer.
  2. Open FileSort and click Select folder. Choose that folder.
  3. Under Sort by, choose Month.
  4. Check the list, then press Sort files.

You get folders such as 2026-08, 2026-09 and 2026-10, each holding the photos from that month. Videos in the same folder are sorted the same way.

Sort a folder now, free

Important: date modified, not date taken

FileSort files each photo by the date the file was last modified. It does not read the "date taken" stored inside the photo.

For photos copied straight from a camera or phone the two dates are normally the same, so the result is what you expect. They can differ when:

  • you edited or rotated a photo later;
  • the photos came from a messaging app or a cloud download, which often stamps them with the download date;
  • the camera clock was wrong.

Check the list before you press Sort. If many photos are headed for the current month, their dates were reset and sorting by month will not help for those.

Your photos stay private

The sorting is done by your browser on your own computer. No photo is uploaded, and there is no account. This is the difference between FileSort and photo services that need your library in their cloud.

Other ways to split up photos

  • By format: choose Extension to separate jpg, png, heic and raw files.
  • Photos from everything else: choose File type to pull images and videos out of a mixed folder into Images and Videos.

Common questions

Can I do this on an iPhone?

Phones cannot let a website rearrange files. On a phone FileSort gives you a sorted .zip copy of the files you select. For a whole library, do it on a computer.

Will it change or compress my photos?

No. Files are moved exactly as they are.
